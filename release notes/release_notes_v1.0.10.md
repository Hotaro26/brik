## brik v1.0.10 — Ascended Release

### Assistive Quick Controls Configuration

- Dedicated Assistive Touch Settings Window: Accessible from Miscellaneous settings, providing full control over the floating quick controls ball.
- Master Activation Toggle: Enable or disable the floating ball across kiosk modes (Zen Mode, Wind Down, and Focus Mode).
- Inactivity Auto-Hide Selector: Configure fading and dimming delay (2s, 3s, 5s, or Always On).
- Quick Controls Tile Selection: Customize visible tiles inside the expanded panel, including Wi-Fi, Mobile Data, Hotspot, Flashlight, Bluetooth, and Do Not Disturb (DND).
- Notification Summary in Quick Controls: Toggle showing a notification summary pill inside the expanded quick controls card, with selectable Badge Count and Banner Preview display styles.
- System Access Shortcuts: Direct access to system Notification Listener and Notification Policy settings.
- Dynamic Layout Adaptation: The expanded Quick Controls panel now adapts its dimensions to match the number of active tiles and notification components.

### Kiosk Launcher Settings

- Dedicated Kiosk Configuration Screen: Accessible from Miscellaneous settings to govern lockdown behaviors during kiosk sessions.
- Incoming Calls Window: Toggle displaying an incoming call window overlay without breaking kiosk focus lockdown.
- Emergency Dialer Shortcut: Option to display a direct emergency dialer shortcut on the kiosk desktop.
- Auto-Resume Session: Automatically re-locks into kiosk mode once a phone call concludes.
- System Lockdown Preferences: Configure notification shade restrictions and system gesture allowances.
- Exit Hold Duration: Configurable screen hold duration (1s, 2s, 3s, or 5s) required to reveal exit controls on the kiosk background.

### Pull-Down Refresh Animations

- Multi-Style Canvas Vector Indicators: Choose from distinct pull-down indicator animations including Smiley (default), Cat, Butterfly, Books, Man in Lotus, or Random Mixed mode.
- Interactive Preview: Live animated indicator preview card in the Miscellaneous settings window.

### Navigation Architecture

- Multi-Level Sub-Screen Stack: Implemented a navigation history stack supporting deep navigation into nested sub-screens with seamless predictive back gesture transitions.
